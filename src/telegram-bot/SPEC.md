## Purpose
[2026-10-31 10:12 SGT] The parent's main way in: receives nudge messages with ✅ Done and 😴 Not today (rule 3.5, owned by the [Nudge engine](../nudge-engine/SPEC.md)), lets the parent log any time, and passes photos to the [Photo check](../photo-check/SPEC.md). It is a **thin layer**: the decisions live in the Nudge engine, and the data in the [Datastore](../datastore/SPEC.md). Telegram is reached with plain web requests (no Telegram library), through a thin web address in `api/` (rule S3).

- **5.1** [2026-11-12 20:59 SGT] Parents can log **any time, for today**: tap ✅ Done on a nudge message; tap a behaviour on the **persistent keyboard** (one button per chosen behaviour, up to 3; one button is fine); use **/log** in the Menu (the same behaviour choice, no day question); or **type** what they did. A keyboard tap while a nudge for that behaviour is still waiting **answers that nudge**: the nudge keeps its words and loses its buttons, and the receipt is a new message (rule 5.10). **Typed text** is matched only when no other prompt is waiting; keyboard labels, commands and button taps are never matched. Claude matches it to the habits with the child's name removed: a **chosen habit** → the bot asks "Did you do ...?" (✅ Yes, log it / ❌ No) and logs it only on Yes, the words becoming the log's line; **No** → saved as a reflection; an **unchosen habit** or a **family moment** → saved as a **reflection** (rule 5.4); **two matches** → the bot asks which; **unrelated text, or Claude fails** → nothing saved, fixed message "I couldn't find a match. Tap one on the keyboard to log today." (a failure is recorded, rule 1.3). *(decided 28 Oct, changed 8 Nov twice, changed 9 Nov: typed messages; changed 12 Nov: the receipt is a new message)* Tested by: tests/telegram-bot-done.test.ts (✅ Done on a nudge, thin slice 1) [2026-11-12 20:59 SGT]. The keyboard, /log and typed text are not built yet.
- **5.3** Optional **description** (up to 280 characters) = the Hooked "Investment". Over 280: the bot asks to shorten it. Shown in the web app and snapshot, and given to Claude for future nudges. *(decided 24 Oct)* Not yet tested (nothing built; the 280 limit per nudge is checked by the Nudge repository tests).
- **5.4** [2026-11-11 17:15 SGT] **Today only:** a log is always for today; there is no yesterday option. A tap on an old nudge's ✅ Done after midnight saves nothing (pop-up: "Reminder has expired. Tap a behaviour on the keyboard to log today."); typed text about yesterday is not logged. **A behaviour not chosen** (by `/log` or typed) is **saved as a reflection, with thanks**: never a habit log, never toward targets. A `/log` tap on an unchosen habit has no words yet, so the bot thanks the parent and offers "Add how you felt"; the reflection is saved once a line is added, and nothing is saved if no line comes (owner confirmed, 11 Nov). A **reflection** is a saved line, stored like a moment's line, counted under "Feelings logged" in the Wrap Up and never toward days logged, targets, streaks or steady weeks. **Twice in a day:** counts once for targets and streaks and gets a special congratulation (receipt: "✅ Saved: <behaviour>, again today. It counts once for your week."); Undo applies. *(decided 28 Oct, changed 9 Nov: today only, unchosen = reflection, repeat celebrated; changed 11 Nov: the /log detail)* Tested by: tests/nudge-engine-due.test.ts and tests/nudge-engine-planner.test.ts (how repeat logs affect what is due). The today-only check: tests/variable-rewards-week.test.ts (a log for any day but today is refused, in Singapore time). Storing reflections: src/datastore/supabase/tests/datastore-tables.test.sql (T47), and logs for today only (T21). The bot side for Done (today only, expired pop-up, saved once): tests/telegram-bot-done.test.ts [2026-11-11 18:17 SGT]; unchosen and repeat logs are not built yet.
- **3.14** [2026-11-12 23:07 SGT] **Sign-up screen 8 shows the plan as one summary**, titled "From what you chose, your reminders are:" (owner, 12 Nov): how often for each behaviour picked (rule 2.5 defaults), "A wrap up for the week will be sent on Sunday at [time]." (rule 3.12) and "Change these anytime in Settings.", with **one button, OK**. - **5.6** [2026-11-11 17:15 SGT] **Weekly connection question** ("How connected did you feel with your child? (1 = not much, 5 = very)", 1 to 5, metric 10). **When:** on Sunday **at the parent's Wrap Up time** (rule 3.12), as **its own message**: right after the Wrap Up summary if the parent had any activity that week, otherwise **on its own**. **Words:** the **same fixed words every week, never written by Claude**, with no mention of a quiet week, and a fixed grey line "Question expires in 24 hrs" (fixed text, not a ticking timer). **Not sent while paused** (the one Pause switch, rule 3.12). **Never counts** toward the 3-a-day cap (rule 3.4). **Answering:** one tap, **within 24 hours of sending**, no reminder. The bot then edits the message (Telegram shows no parent bubble): the buttons go and the line reads "Answered: 4, quite connected", using the labels 1 Disconnected, 2 Slightly connected, 3 Moderately connected, 4 Quite connected, 5 Strongly connected; tapped after midnight Sunday it reads "Answered (week of Mon 2 Nov)". The stored value is the number. An answer counts for **the week it was asked**. Any extra tap is ignored and the first answer counts (database backstop, rule 8.6); the answer can't be changed. **Expiry:** after 24 hours the bot edits the message once (buttons removed, "Question expired"); a failed edit is recorded as a failure, and a late answer is still refused; a stale tap is not saved and gets a pop-up, "This question has closed." *(decided 1 Nov, changed 9 Nov twice, changed 11 Nov: 24 hours, labels, own message)* Tested by: src/datastore/supabase/tests/datastore-tables.test.sql (T30 the first answer counts; T45 its own message, once a week; T46 refused with no question or after 24 hours), tests/datastore-windows.test.ts (24 hours). Sending and editing the message are not built yet (Telegram bot).
- **5.7** [2026-11-08 23:42 SGT] **The Menu.** The Menu button next to the message box offers `/mypage` (open My page, the parent's dashboard on the Web app), `/settings` (open Settings, which lives inside My page), `/log` (log a behaviour: the same choice as the persistent keyboard, rule 5.1), `/pause` (pause reminders: links to Settings), `/resume` (restart reminders: confirm with buttons, done in Telegram) and `/help` (how this works). Commands never take extra words. Plain words without the slash (for example "pause" or "resume") work too, to reduce friction. *(decided 8 Nov, changed 8 Nov)* Not yet tested (nothing built).
- **5.8** [2026-11-11 17:28 SGT] **Undo.** Under the receipt of a keyboard log (rule 5.1), an **Undo** button lets the parent mark that one log as **undone** within **30 seconds** of logging (`UNDO_WINDOW_SECONDS` in `src/settings.ts`, rule 1.4). The log row is **kept and flagged**, never deleted; its "how you felt" line is blanked and its photo deleted, even a checked one (owner, 9 and 11 Nov); an event is written. An undone log never counts toward days, targets, streaks, steady weeks or the Weekly Wrap Up; the bot replies "Undone. It won't count." After the window, the bot edits the message to remove the button. A typed log confirmed with Yes has no Undo. *(decided 8 Nov, changed 11 Nov: clean-up and database checks)* Tested by: src/datastore/supabase/tests/datastore-tables.test.sql (T49: keyboard only, within the window, once, line blanked, photo deleted; T53: refused if the settings version has no window), tests/datastore-windows.test.ts (30 seconds). The button and the reply are not built yet (Telegram bot).
- **5.9** [2026-11-11 17:57 SGT] **Only Telegram can talk to the bot.** Every request to the webhook must carry our secret in Telegram's secret-token header (set when the webhook is registered); any other request is refused and nothing is saved or sent (owner, 9 Nov privacy decision). *(decided 9 Nov, written and built 11 Nov)* Tested by: tests/telegram-bot-webhook.test.ts (refused without the secret or with a wrong one; nothing saved or sent).
- **5.10** [2026-11-12 20:59 SGT] **A reply to a nudge never replaces it.** When the parent answers a nudge (✅ Done, 😴 Not today, or a keyboard tap that answers a waiting nudge, rule 5.1), the nudge **keeps its words and loses its buttons** (first, so it can't be tapped again), and the receipt or the comforting note arrives as a **new message** below it. **Once a nudge is answered**, a tap on any of its buttons saves nothing more and shows the pop-up "Already answered." (a second ✅ Done keeps "Already saved."; review finding, 12 Nov), so its buttons can't record two answers if removing them failed. Once the answer is saved, a Telegram failure while removing the buttons or sending the reply is written to the log but never shown as "didn't save" (rule 1.3: that would be untrue). Messages that only lose a button or update a ✓ (the agreement, Undo, onboarding picks) and the weekly connection question (rule 5.6, answered and expired lines) are still edited in place; a nudge sent but not recorded is still edited into an honest note (rule 1.3). *(decided 12 Nov, owner, after UAT U11: a nudge that disappears is not intuitive; changed 12 Nov: "Already answered.")* Tested by: tests/telegram-bot-done.test.ts (Done and Not today keep the nudge and send a new message, in that order; a failure to remove the buttons or to send the reply after saving never says "didn't save"; after Done or Not today, another tap shows "Already answered." and saves nothing; a failed save leaves the buttons). Database backstop for two taps at the same instant: not built yet (known issue, migration 007). The keyboard case is not built yet.
