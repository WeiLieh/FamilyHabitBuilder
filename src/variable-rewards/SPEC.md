# Variable rewards: module spec

[2026-10-31 10:05 SGT] The **current, agreed rules** of the Variable rewards module (what parents get after logging). Rule IDs are permanent. The working design, with the reasoning, the universe of rewards and the decisions still open (D5 to D10), is in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md). The dated history of each decision is in the change notes of the master spec, [docs/SPEC.md](../../docs/SPEC.md). The owner's original v0.1 rewards spec is history: `docs/archive/variable-rewards-spec-v0.1-original.md`.

## Purpose
[2026-10-31 10:05 SGT] Make logging a bonding moment feel noticed and worthwhile without turning family time into points: rewards are **informational** ("here is what you did"), never **controlling** ("do this to earn points").

- **7.3** **Streaks are weekly** (D3): one unit for everyone, the week (Monday to Sunday). The headline is "days this week"; the streak counts **steady weeks** (rule 7.6), in total and in a row. A short week never gets a "broken streak" message. No repair pass in the MVP. Replaces the earlier per-behaviour streaks. *(decided 24 Oct, changed 29 Oct)* Not yet tested (nothing built).
- **7.4** **Variable rewards need a full design session:** every reward category needs its rules, its content and its variety (parked in docs/PRODUCT-BACKLOG.md; in progress in DESIGN.md). *(decided 29 Oct)* Not yet tested (a design task, not code).

Replies never count toward the 3-a-day cap. *(decided 29 Oct)* Tested by: tests/telegram-bot-done.test.ts (the fixed receipt replaces the nudge; no Claude reply in thin slice 1) [2026-11-11 18:17 SGT]. - **7.7** [2026-11-11 17:15 SGT] **Saving a moment from the Weekly Wrap Up** (D8): the parent may save **one photo and/or one line about any moment that week**, even outside the 9 nudges, by tapping **Add a moment from this week** on the Wrap Up summary (the button stays after the connection question is answered, and works until the next Wrap Up). The bot replies "Send a photo, or type one line about a moment from this week." with a fixed line "Upload within 24 hrs", and **waits 24 hours** (a starting number in `src/settings.ts`). Other prompts win while it waits; keyboard labels, commands and button taps are never saved as moments; the window closes once one photo and one line are saved, or at the next Wrap Up. After 24 hours a late photo or line is not saved: "Moment has expired. Tap Add a moment from this week to try again." with its own button, which starts a fresh 24 hours. A moment is an **album entry, not a log**: it never changes targets, streaks or steady weeks, but it counts toward the "First Memory" badge and under Feelings logged or Photos in the Wrap Up (rule 7.8). **No AI photo check** (it could be any photo; rules 6.1 and 6.2 are for logs). Receipt: "✅ Saved to your album." (fixed text, no Claude reply). Moments are **not sent to Claude**. **Photo consent** (rule 5.5) at the parent's first photo ever: "[Photo consent wording, awaiting your PDPA reviewer.] We save your photos so you can see your weekly wrap up." with **OK** and **Don't save my photos**; Don't save: the photo is stored nowhere, "No problem. That photo wasn't saved. You can type one line instead.", and consent is asked again at the next photo. **No real family sends photos until photo consent and "delete my data" are built** (`docs/KNOWN-ISSUES.md`). *(decided 31 Oct, changed 11 Nov: the 9 Nov Chat design)* Tested by: src/datastore/supabase/tests/datastore-tables.test.sql (T48: the latest Wrap Up's week only, one photo and one line, the photo never checked; fix 2 rewritten: consent), tests/datastore-windows.test.ts (24 hours). The 24-hour wait itself is checked by the app (not built yet).
