## Purpose
[2026-10-31 10:05 SGT] Make logging a bonding moment feel noticed and worthwhile without turning family time into points: rewards are **informational** ("here is what you did"), never **controlling** ("do this to earn points").

## Rules at a glance
7.1 MVP rewards · 7.2 no XP or levels; special reply for unprompted logs · 7.3 weekly streaks · 7.4 full design still needed · 7.5 receipt, then Claude's reply · 7.6 steady week, judged at midnight Sunday · 7.7 saving a moment from the Wrap Up · 7.8 weekly and monthly snapshots

## Rules
- **7.1** [2026-10-31 17:59 SGT] **MVP rewards** (from [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md)): 1 validation reply after each log, 2 varied ideas (a fresh example in each nudge message, rule 3.13; no separate surprise cards, no "Another idea" button), 3 weekly streak, 4 one-tap logging, 6 family memory album, with the **weekly snapshot and album prompt combined into the Weekly Wrap Up** (rule 3.12) and a monthly "last month" section in the first Wrap Up of each month (rule 7.8), 10 three milestone badges. Unlocks and a repair pass are out of the MVP. Album entries from the Wrap Up: rule 7.7. *(decided 24 Oct, changed 29 Oct and 31 Oct)* Not yet tested (nothing built).
- **7.2** **No XP or levels in the MVP** (D1). No random surprise bonus either. An **unprompted log** (rule 5.2) gets a **special validation reply** noticing the parent did it on their own. *(decided 24 Oct, changed 29 Oct)* Not yet tested (nothing built).
- **7.3** **Streaks are weekly** (D3): one unit for everyone, the week (Monday to Sunday). The headline is "days this week"; the streak counts **steady weeks** (rule 7.6), in total and in a row. A short week never gets a "broken streak" message. No repair pass in the MVP. Replaces the earlier per-behaviour streaks. *(decided 24 Oct, changed 29 Oct)* Not yet tested (nothing built).
- **7.4** **Variable rewards need a full design session:** every reward category needs its rules, its content and its variety (parked in docs/PRODUCT-BACKLOG.md; in progress in DESIGN.md). *(decided 29 Oct)* Not yet tested (a design task, not code).
- **7.5** [2026-11-12 20:54 SGT] **Validation reply after every log** (D2): first an honest **receipt** ("✅ Saved: Showing affection today."), then a warm reply **written by Claude** naming what the parent did. If Claude fails, the receipt stands and the failure is recorded. Replies never count toward the 3-a-day cap. (no comma: the wording the owner confirmed on 8 Nov, confirmed again 11 Nov) *(decided 29 Oct)* Tested by: tests/telegram-bot-done.test.ts (the fixed receipt is a new message below the nudge, rule 5.10; no Claude reply in thin slice 1) [2026-11-12 20:54 SGT]. Claude's reply is not built yet; the cap part is covered by the Nudge engine's rule 3.4 tests.
- **7.6** [2026-11-11 17:15 SGT] **Steady week** (D4) = every behaviour the parent chose met its weekly bar, with some slack: every day = 6 of 7 days; every 2 days = 3 days; deliberate once a week = 1; twice a week = 2. The week a parent joins doesn't count against them (their streak starts with their first full week). Picks in place on Sunday are the ones judged. **A week is judged when it closes at midnight on Sunday** (D5b). Since 9 Nov logging is **for today only** (rule 5.4), so no log can arrive for a closed week; the "Saved for Sunday" receipt is retired. *(decided 29 Oct, changed 31 Oct, changed 9 Nov: Monday-for-Sunday receipt retired; bars are starting values, tune after the trial)* Tested by: tests/variable-rewards-week.test.ts (today-only logs count for their week; any other day is refused). The steady-week bars are not yet tested (nothing built).
- **7.7** [2026-11-11 17:15 SGT] **Saving a moment from the Weekly Wrap Up** (D8): the parent may save **one photo and/or one line about any moment that week**, even outside the 9 nudges, by tapping **Add a moment from this week** on the Wrap Up summary (the button stays after the connection question is answered, and works until the next Wrap Up). The bot replies "Send a photo, or type one line about a moment from this week." with a fixed line "Upload within 24 hrs", and **waits 24 hours** (a starting number in `src/settings.ts`). Other prompts win while it waits; keyboard labels, commands and button taps are never saved as moments; the window closes once one photo and one line are saved, or at the next Wrap Up. After 24 hours a late photo or line is not saved: "Moment has expired. Tap Add a moment from this week to try again." with its own button, which starts a fresh 24 hours. A moment is an **album entry, not a log**: it never changes targets, streaks or steady weeks, but it counts toward the "First Memory" badge and under Feelings logged or Photos in the Wrap Up (rule 7.8). **No AI photo check** (it could be any photo; rules 6.1 and 6.2 are for logs). Receipt: "✅ Saved to your album." (fixed text, no Claude reply). Moments are **not sent to Claude**. **Photo consent** (rule 5.5) at the parent's first photo ever: "[Photo consent wording, awaiting your PDPA reviewer.] We save your photos so you can see your weekly wrap up." with **OK** and **Don't save my photos**; Don't save: the photo is stored nowhere, "No problem. That photo wasn't saved. You can type one line instead.", and consent is asked again at the next photo. **No real family sends photos until photo consent and "delete my data" are built** (`docs/KNOWN-ISSUES.md`). *(decided 31 Oct, changed 11 Nov: the 9 Nov Chat design)* Tested by: src/datastore/supabase/tests/datastore-tables.test.sql (T48: the latest Wrap Up's week only, one photo and one line, the photo never checked; fix 2 rewritten: consent), tests/datastore-windows.test.ts (24 hours). The 24-hour wait itself is checked by the app (not built yet).
- **7.8** [2026-11-09 21:48 SGT] **Snapshots** (D10). **Weekly = the Weekly Wrap Up** (rule 3.12): **two messages**. (1) **The summary**: "Your week, <first name>" with **Habits logged** (days logged this week so far), **Feelings logged** (lines added with "Add how you felt", lines saved as moments, and reflections) and **Photos** (photos saved this week, from logs and moments), and two buttons: **Add a moment from this week** (rule 7.7) and **View wrap up** (opens My page, rule 9.1). (2) **The weekly connection question** (rule 5.6). A parent with no activity that week (no log, reflection or moment) gets no summary, only the connection question on its own (rules 3.4 and 5.6). **Steady weeks and the monthly view are not in the Wrap Up:** they live on **My page** (rule 9.1); the "Your streak starts next week" line is dropped (owner, 9 Nov). A **paused** parent gets nothing on Sunday but still sees everything in the Web app. All numbers come from real data, never estimated (rule 1.3). **Words:** a **fixed template filled with real numbers, never written by Claude**. *(decided 31 Oct, changed 8 Nov, changed 9 Nov three times: fixed template; two messages and new summary items)* Not yet tested (built with the Wrap Up's words, step 4).
