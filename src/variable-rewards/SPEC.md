# Variable rewards: module spec

[2026-10-31 10:05 SGT] The **current, agreed rules** of the Variable rewards module (what parents get after logging). Rule IDs are permanent. The working design, with the reasoning, the universe of rewards and the decisions still open (D5 to D10), is in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md). The dated history of each decision is in the change notes of the master spec, [docs/SPEC.md](../../docs/SPEC.md). The owner's original v0.1 rewards spec is history: `docs/archive/variable-rewards-spec-v0.1-original.md`.

## Purpose
[2026-10-31 10:05 SGT] Make logging a bonding moment feel noticed and worthwhile without turning family time into points: rewards are **informational** ("here is what you did"), never **controlling** ("do this to earn points").

## Rules
- **7.1** [2026-10-31 17:59 SGT] **MVP rewards** (from [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md)): 1 validation reply after each log, 2 varied ideas (a fresh example in each nudge message, rule 3.13; no separate surprise cards, no "Another idea" button), 3 weekly streak, 4 one-tap logging, 6 family memory album, with the **weekly snapshot and album prompt combined into the Weekly Wrap Up** (rule 3.12) and a monthly "last month" section in the first Wrap Up of each month (rule 7.8), 10 three milestone badges. Unlocks and a repair pass are out of the MVP. Album entries from the Wrap Up: rule 7.7. *(decided 24 Oct, changed 29 Oct and 31 Oct)* Not yet tested (nothing built).
- SUPERSEDED [2026-10-31 17:59 SGT] **7.1** **MVP rewards** (from [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md)): 1 validation reply after each log, 2 varied ideas (a fresh example in each nudge message, rule 3.13; no separate surprise cards, no "Another idea" button), 3 weekly streak, 4 one-tap logging, 6 family memory album, with the **weekly snapshot and album prompt combined into the Weekly Wrap Up** (rule 3.12) and a monthly snapshot (timing pending D10), 10 three milestone badges. Unlocks and a repair pass are out of the MVP. D8 to D10 open. *(decided 24 Oct, changed 29 Oct and 31 Oct)* Not yet tested (nothing built).
- SUPERSEDED [2026-10-31 17:52 SGT] **7.1** **MVP rewards** (from [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md)): 1 validation reply after each log, 2 varied ideas (surprise), 3 weekly streak, 4 one-tap logging, 6 family memory album **including weekly and monthly snapshots**, 10 three milestone badges. Unlocks are out of the MVP. Details under review (D5 to D10 open). *(decided 24 Oct, changed 29 Oct)* Not yet tested (nothing built).
- **7.2** **No XP or levels in the MVP** (D1). No random surprise bonus either. An **unprompted log** (rule 5.2) gets a **special validation reply** noticing the parent did it on their own. *(decided 24 Oct, changed 29 Oct)* Not yet tested (nothing built).
- **7.3** **Streaks are weekly** (D3): one unit for everyone, the week (Monday to Sunday). The headline is "days this week"; the streak counts **steady weeks** (rule 7.6), in total and in a row. A short week never gets a "broken streak" message. No repair pass in the MVP. Replaces the earlier per-behaviour streaks. *(decided 24 Oct, changed 29 Oct)* Not yet tested (nothing built).
- **7.4** **Variable rewards need a full design session:** every reward category needs its rules, its content and its variety (parked in docs/PRODUCT-BACKLOG.md; in progress in DESIGN.md). *(decided 29 Oct)* Not yet tested (a design task, not code).
- **7.5** **Validation reply after every log** (D2): first an honest **receipt** ("✅ Saved: Showing affection, today."), then a warm reply **written by Claude** naming what the parent did. If Claude fails, the receipt stands and the failure is recorded. Replies never count toward the 3-a-day cap. *(decided 29 Oct)* Not yet tested (nothing built; the cap part is covered by the Nudge engine's rule 3.4 tests).
- **7.6** [2026-10-31 17:52 SGT] **Steady week** (D4) = every behaviour the parent chose met its weekly bar, with some slack: every day = 6 of 7 days; every 2 days = 3 days; deliberate once a week = 1; twice a week = 2. The week a parent joins doesn't count against them (their streak starts with their first full week). Picks in place on Sunday are the ones judged. **A week is judged when it closes at midnight on Sunday** (D5b): a log made on Monday for Sunday (allowed by rule 5.4) is saved in the history and album but counts toward neither week, and the receipt says so honestly, e.g. "Saved for Sunday. Last week has closed, so it's in your album but not in last week's count." *(decided 29 Oct, changed 31 Oct; bars are starting values, tune after the trial)* Tested by: tests/variable-rewards-week.test.ts (when a week closes). The steady-week bars are not yet tested (nothing built).
- SUPERSEDED [2026-10-31 17:52 SGT] **7.6** **Steady week** (D4) = every behaviour the parent chose met its weekly bar, with some slack: every day = 6 of 7 days; every 2 days = 3 days; deliberate once a week = 1; twice a week = 2. The week a parent joins doesn't count against them (their streak starts with their first full week). Picks in place on Sunday are the ones judged. *(decided 29 Oct; bars are starting values, tune after the trial)* Not yet tested (nothing built).
- **7.7** [2026-10-31 17:59 SGT] **Saving a moment from the Weekly Wrap Up** (D8): the parent may save **one photo and/or one line about any moment that week**, even outside the 9 nudges, until the next Wrap Up. It is an **album entry, not a log**: it never changes targets, streaks or steady weeks, but it does count toward the "First Memory" badge. Photo rules 5.5 apply; **no real family sends photos until photo consent and "delete my data" are built** (`docs/KNOWN-ISSUES.md`). *(decided 31 Oct)* Not yet tested (nothing built).
- **7.8** [2026-10-31 17:59 SGT] **Snapshots** (D10). **Weekly = the Weekly Wrap Up** (rule 3.12), showing: (1) days logged this week so far; (2) steady weeks so far, in total and in a row, up to last week; (3) photos and lines saved this week; (4) a one-tap link to the Web app; (5) "Want to save one moment from this week?" (rule 7.7). **Monthly** = a "last month" section added to the **first Weekly Wrap Up sent in each calendar month** (no separate message, nothing counted toward the cap; a parent who turned the Wrap Up off sees it in the Web app only). All numbers come from real data, never estimated (rule 1.3). *(decided 31 Oct)* Not yet tested (built with the Wrap Up's words, step 4).
