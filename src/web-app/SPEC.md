## Rules
- **9.1** [2026-11-08 22:34 SGT] Plain, unstyled pages that show the right data; **the look comes from the owner later**. **My page** is the parent's dashboard (`/mypage` in Telegram). **Settings lives inside My page**: behaviours, how often (rule 2.5), times, Weekly Wrap Up and **pause**, reusing the Telegram sign-up conversations; these are the same settings as sign-up screen 8 (rule 3.14). "Delete my data" and **"delete a photo"** live here too. `/pause` and `/settings` in Telegram link here (the pause speed bump); resume stays in Telegram (rule 5.7). *(decided 24 Oct, changed 8 Nov)* Not yet tested (nothing built).
  - [2026-11-14 22:24 SGT] Addition (owner, 14 Nov): rule 9.1 stands; My page is now organised as four tabs (rule 9.2), and Settings is tab 4.
- **9.2** [2026-11-14 22:24 SGT] **My page has four tabs** in a bar at the bottom; tapping a tab opens its own page. (1) **Week so far**: "reassure me, let me relive it" (rule 9.4); it also holds "days logged this week" and, behind a button near the bottom, the older weeks and their photos (rule 9.7). (2) **Habits so far**: "show me we're growing" (not designed yet). (3) **Rewards**: "give me my rewards"; shown **greyed out and disabled** for now, and the layout must leave room to switch it on later. (4) **Settings**: "let me adjust it, let me feel safe"; a placeholder for now, to hold pause, delete a photo or line, delete my data, sign out, reminder times, children and consents (rule 9.1). The word "album" is not used in the product (the backlog item "Download my memories" replaces it). *(decided 14 Nov, owner, design chat)* Not yet tested (nothing built).
- **9.3** [2026-11-14 22:24 SGT] **Sign-in:** a **one-tap link from Telegram** (Option A) opens My page signed in, with **Telegram's own login** as the fallback. A sign-in lasts **30 days** (a number for `src/settings.ts`, rule 1.4). No email address or phone number is asked for. *(decided 14 Nov, owner, design chat)* Not yet tested (nothing built).
- **9.4** [2026-11-14 22:24 SGT] **The Week so far page** (layout 1; weeks run Monday to Sunday). Top to bottom: (1) a header, "Hi [parent's name]" and "[9 to 15 Nov] with [child] and [child]"; (2) **the week's moment** (rule 7.7) with the parent's one-line caption and a dustbin; with no moment, the next block moves to the top; (3) **"[N] days logged this week"**: a seven-day strip (M T W T F S S, a tick on each logged day) and the log rows below it, in **one box**; each row shows the habit, the child, the day, the parent's optional line, the photo if any (as a small picture; a row without a photo is text only) and a dustbin; rows are **newest first**; (4) **"[N] reflection(s) this week"**: each reflection in its own box, the parent's words **exactly as typed, in italics, inside double quotes**, with a dustbin; (5) the **connection card** (rule 9.5); (6) the **older weeks button** (rule 9.7); (7) the tab bar (rule 9.2). **Days logged** counts only days with a habit log: a reflection is never a habit log and never counts toward days logged, targets, streaks or steady weeks (rules 5.4, 7.6). Section titles sit **outside and above** their boxes, at the same size as the page's titles. The **dustbin** has one look everywhere: no border, a tap area big enough for a thumb, beside every photo, line and moment that can be deleted. *(decided 14 Nov, owner, design chat)* Not yet tested (nothing built).
- **9.5** [2026-11-14 22:24 SGT] **The connection card** (on Week so far). Top to bottom: the weekly question (rule 5.6; exact wording open, see above); "[4]/5 : For week [9 to 15 Nov]"; **five large circles** across the width, numbered 1 to 5, "not much" under 1 and "very" under 5, filled up to the answer; a divider; "[3.3]/5: Average over the 3 weeks before"; then **four bars**, each with its rating on top and its label under it: the three earlier weeks' Mondays ("19 Oct", "26 Oct", "2 Nov") and "This week" (picked out). The average is the **plain mean** of the 3 earlier answers. **Which week:** the answer is given on Sunday at the Wrap Up and saved against **that week's Monday** (one per parent per week, rules 5.6 and 8.6), so the card shows the week just ending; before Sunday's answer it shows the "not asked yet" title. The title above the card is a default awaiting the owner's wording (see above). Every number comes from real answers, never estimated (rule 1.3). *(decided 14 Nov, owner, design chat; the titles are a default)* Not yet tested (nothing built).
- **9.6** [2026-11-14 22:24 SGT] **Deleting on My page leaves the log.** A dustbin deletes **only** the photo, the line or the moment beside it; the habit log stays and still counts (like Undo keeps its row, rule 5.8). A deleted photo leaves **no copy anywhere** (owner, 9 Nov). The same rule applies on Week so far and on an older week's page. Each delete needs its own narrow Datastore function (rule 8.4 today lets only "delete my data" remove rows, so whether a deleted reflection or moment line is removed or blanked is still open; proposal of 14 Nov in [src/datastore/SPEC.md](../datastore/SPEC.md), awaiting the owner). *(decided 14 Nov, owner, design chat)* Not yet tested (nothing built).
- **9.7** [2026-11-14 22:24 SGT] **Older weeks.** **The list (layout 2):** a button near the bottom of Week so far **adds 4 older weeks** below this week, on the same page (how many is a number for `src/settings.ts`, rule 1.4). The button's label is **worked out from what the tap will load**, so the two always match: "Show 4 older weeks - back to 12 Oct"; when fewer remain, "Show 1 more week - 5 to 11 Oct". Weeks are listed **newest first**. Each older week is a **folded card**: its dates as a title above the box ("2 to 8 Nov"), a count line ("3 days logged, 1 reflection"; without reflections "3 days logged: 3 photos", "1 day logged: 1 photo"; days = days with a habit log), and a box with **one tile per thing logged**, oldest first within the week: a photo tile for a log with a photo, and a **reflection tile** the same size, with a quote mark and the first few words in italics (variant A). There is a chevron and **the whole card is a link** to that week's page; no dustbins and no captions in the list. The list loads as the parent asks for it, and tiles load as small pictures. **An older week's own page (layout 3, Option B):** the only control is a **Back** button (the phone's back also works); **the tab bar is hidden**. The header shows the week's dates, "with [child] and [child]" and the same count line. Then the week **day by day, oldest first** (Monday to Sunday): a day title, a small line "[habit] · [child]" or "Reflection", then the photo with the parent's line and a dustbin, or the reflection in italics inside quotes in its own box with a dustbin. Days with nothing logged are not shown. **No day strip, no weekly moment and no connection card** on this page. Deleting follows rule 9.6. *(decided 14 Nov, owner, design chat)* Not yet tested (nothing built).
