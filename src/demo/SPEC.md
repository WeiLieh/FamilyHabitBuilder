## Purpose
[2026-11-24 23:31 SGT] Let the owner preview all the built functionality to an audience in a few minutes: the real sign-up, each chosen habit's reminder once, the photo, "Add how you felt", the Sunday Wrap Up with Add a moment and View wrap up, and the 1 to 5 question. The demo changes **only the pacing**: every reply behaves exactly as it does for a real parent (owner, 24 Nov).

## Rules
- **12.1** [2026-11-24 23:31 SGT] **Only the owner.** `/demo`, `/next` and `/demo stop` work only from the owner's Telegram account (an allow-list in Vercel's settings, never in the code). From anyone else they are unknown commands ("Unrecognised text."). *(decided 24 Nov, owner, Q7)* Not yet tested (nothing built).
- **12.2** [2026-11-24 23:31 SGT] **Starting.** `/demo` deletes any earlier demo parent and all its data (rule 12.7), creates a **fresh demo parent** linked to the owner, and starts the **real sign-up** at screen 2. The only difference from a real sign-up: **no invite code**. The recording agreement is shown and recorded for the demo parent; photo consent is asked at the first photo, as usual. *(decided 24 Nov, owner, Q6 A, Q10)* Not yet tested (nothing built).
- **12.3** [2026-11-24 23:31 SGT] **The switch.** While demo mode is on, everything the owner sends or taps belongs to the demo parent. The owner's **real reminders, Wrap Up and connection question pause** (nothing is sent to the real account). `/mypage` and View wrap up open the **demo parent's** My page. `/demo stop` switches back. While on, a message that isn't part of the demo gets a gentle note: "You're in demo mode. Type /demo stop to go back." `/next` and `/demo stop` are added to the Menu only while a demo runs. *(decided 24 Nov, owner, Q1 A, Q9, risk accepted)* Not yet tested (nothing built).
- **12.4** [2026-11-24 23:31 SGT] **The short week, presenter-paced.** After "You're all set" (and "Start right now", as in rule 3.14's sign-up), nothing more is sent until the owner types **`/next`**. Each `/next` sends the next step: first each chosen habit's reminder **once**, in the order picked (up to 2 quick, then the deliberate one; rule 2.1), then the **Wrap Up summary** (Add a moment, View wrap up), then the **1 to 5 question**. After the question, `/next` says the demo is complete ("That's the whole week. Type /demo stop to finish."). *(decided 24 Nov, owner, Q4, Q12 B, Q13 B, Q14 A)* Not yet tested (nothing built).
- **12.5** [2026-11-24 23:31 SGT] **Same behaviour, only paced.** Every reply works exactly as for a real parent: ✅ Done, 😴 Not today, Undo, the keyboard and its reaction, `/log`, the photo after a log, "Add how you felt", Add a moment, the 1 to 5 tap, View wrap up. Rule 3.6 applies: a habit already logged (for example through "Start right now" or the keyboard) gets **no reminder**, and that `/next` goes on to the following step. Not today stops the remaining reminders, as it stops the rest of a real day; the next `/next` goes on to the Wrap Up. *(decided 24 Nov, owner, Q2, risk accepted)* Not yet tested (nothing built).
- **12.6** [2026-11-24 23:31 SGT] **Everything happens today.** Logs are for today (rule 5.4). The Wrap Up and the 1 to 5 question are sent when `/next` reaches them, whatever the day; the answer counts for this week (rule 5.6). *(decided 24 Nov, owner, Q5)* Not yet tested (nothing built).
- **12.7** [2026-11-24 23:31 SGT] **Demo data is kept apart.** The demo parent is **marked as demo** and is never counted in Telemetry, the success metrics or experiments, and the 10-minute alarm never sends it anything. `/demo stop` (or a new `/demo`) deletes the demo parent and all its data, **photo files first** (rule 8.5), as "delete my data" would (rule 1.5). *(decided 24 Nov, owner, Q8 A)* Not yet tested (nothing built).
