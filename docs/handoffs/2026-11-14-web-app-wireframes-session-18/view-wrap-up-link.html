Where "View wrap up" in Telegram takes you

A wireframe of where things go, not the look. The Wrap Up for 9 to 15 Nov is sent on Sunday 15 Nov at 8:00 pm. Its button "View wrap up" opens My page. The question: which page?

Proposal: the button always opens the week the Wrap Up is about

Telegram, Sunday 8 pm
Your week, John
Habits logged: 5
Feelings logged: 1
Photos: 2
Add a moment from this week
View wrap up
→
Tapped on Sunday (before midnight)
Week so far
9 to 15 Nov · tab bar shown
It is still this week, so Week so far is that week.
or
Tapped Monday or later
‹ Back · 9 to 15 Nov
that week's own page · tab bar hidden
Week so far is now the new week (16 to 22 Nov), so the button opens 9 to 15 Nov's own page instead. Back goes to Week so far.

The alternative: always Week so far

Tapped Monday or later
Week so far
16 to 22 Nov · nearly empty on a Monday
Simpler, but the parent asked to see last week's wrap up and lands on a new, nearly empty week.
Recommendation: the proposal. The button always shows the week its Wrap Up is about, so it never surprises the parent.
